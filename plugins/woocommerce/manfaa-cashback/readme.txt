Pays Manfaa cashback on WooCommerce orders.

== Description ==

Buyers enter the 6-digit code from their Manfaa app on the cart or at checkout. When the order reaches the status you choose, the sale is posted to Manfaa and the buyer sees the cashback as pending in their app; cancelled and refunded orders reverse it.

* Connect with Manfaa in one click — no keys to copy.
* General rate or per-category pricing mapped to your WooCommerce categories.
* Estimated cashback shown in the cart totals.
* Works with the Cart and Checkout Blocks and the classic shortcode pages; HPOS supported.
